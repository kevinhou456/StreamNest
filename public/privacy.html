Privacy Policy

Bill Manager — Last updated: October 7, 2026

Purpose

Bill Manager is a personal, self-hosted application used to organize household bills and track expiration dates.

Gmail access

With the account owner's permission, Bill Manager requests read-only Gmail access to find billing messages and extract relevant bill information. It does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Information stored

Bill Manager stores selected billing information, account configuration, and authorization credentials on the owner's self-hosted system. It is not a public cloud service.

Sharing

Bill Manager does not sell personal information or share Gmail data with advertisers. Gmail data is used only to provide the bill-management functionality requested by the account owner.

Access and deletion

The owner controls the self-hosted application and can remove stored billing records and revoke Gmail access through Google Account settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes.
